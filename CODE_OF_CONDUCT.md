# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussions focused on the work. Harassment,
personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues, discussions, pull requests or any other
project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **vietanhresearcher@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially,
and the maintainer may remove content or block participants who violate this code.
